Privacy policy

What this site collects, why, and how you stay in control. Last updated on 8 November 2026.

Controller

Mathias Wicht, Canton of Vaud, Switzerland. Contact: wichtmathias0@gmail.com

This site is a personal portfolio. It complies with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for visitors from the European Union, the GDPR.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). On every visit, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address to keep the service secure and running. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. No cookie is set for this.

Analytics cookies

If you accept, the site uses Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd.) to measure visits, pages viewed, visit length, device type and approximate country. IP addresses are anonymised. This data is only used to improve the portfolio.

  • _ga and _ga_*: tell visitors apart, kept for up to 2 years

These cookies are set only after you consent. If you decline, no statistics are collected. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage.

Contact form

Messages sent with the form (name, email, subject, message) go through Web3Forms, a service that forwards them to me by email. To block bots, the form uses hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.), which may collect technical data such as your IP address and mouse movements. See the Web3Forms privacy policy and the hCaptcha privacy policy. Emails I receive are only used to reply and are never shared.

External links

Links to LinkedIn and Instagram lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Just write to wichtmathias0@gmail.com. If needed, you can contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
