Pulse, designing with AI

The full rebranding of a fictional earbuds brand, from positioning to website, using AI as a tool. The goal: show a real before and after, and keep control of every decision.

Year2026
RoleArt direction and site
ContextCPNV, IT workshop
ToolsAI, HTML, CSS, Illustrator

PositioningThe brand

Pulse is an earbuds brand built for focus, somewhere between a pro tool and tech culture. The audience: 15 to 35 year olds, not just developers. The tone: direct, no jargon.

My goal was to work on the project as if the brand had been handed to me, then present the work like an external designer, with a before and after.

IdentityPalette, fonts and logo

Purple and grey with a touch of terminal green, JetBrains Mono for headings and Space Grotesk for body text. For the logo, a P with a pulse line. The first attempt with a glowing halo on black was rejected: the final version is a simple flat mark.

ProductsThe system applied everywhere

Headphones, earbuds, packaging, poster and even an app: the same visual system on every medium. The product visuals are AI-generated from my direction.


SiteWhat moves

On the site, motion serves the brand: a scrolling banner, blocks that appear on scroll, a glowing cursor and product cards that tilt on hover. Everything turns off if the user prefers reduced motion.


ProcessHow I used AI

  • I decide: colours, fonts, logo, every choice comes from a brief I wrote
  • I reject: eight directions were tested then dropped, and I documented them
  • I correct: when the result is too generic, I say so and try again with a reference
  • I check: mobile version, accessibility and animations tested by hand

TakeawaysWhat I learned

AI is fast, but it often suggests the most generic solution. My job is to know when to say no and explain why.
