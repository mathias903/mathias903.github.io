Régimo Lausanne, static web project

Redesign of the website of a property management agency in Lausanne. A full design project: analysis of the company and the market, then Figma mockups for desktop and mobile.

See the mockups
Year2025
RoleAnalysis and mockups
ContextCPNV, 2nd year
ToolsFigma, InDesign

ContextA people-sized agency

Régimo Lausanne manages properties for owners and investors in the cantons of Vaud and Fribourg. In just a few clicks, the site had to show the properties, the services, the team and the job offers.

The target audience: rather young people looking to settle somewhere, and qualified profiles looking for a job in real estate.

AnalysisBefore drawing

  1. 01

    Who are we?

    Defining the company, its role and what the site is for.

  2. 02

    Target audience and expectations

    Reassuring the client: showing the team's expertise and the number of properties, and keeping the usual search criteria (rent or buy, location, price, rooms).

  3. 03

    Competitors

    Comparison with three agencies (deRham, Bernard Nicod, Braun) and three listing sites (immobilier.ch, Immoscout24, Homegate).

  4. 04

    Positioning matrix

    Placing each competitor by design quality and user experience, then deciding what to keep and what to avoid.

BriefThe design brief

MockupsDesktop and mobile

The mockups follow what the analysis showed: a strong red for the identity, photos of people to humanise the company, a selection of properties right on the homepage and a complete footer.



TakeawaysWhat I learned

Analysing the competitors before drawing kept me from going in every direction. Every choice in the mockup has a reason.
