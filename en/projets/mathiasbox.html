MathiasBox Monthly

The e-commerce site of a fictional surprise subscription box company based in Grandson. Built as a group on WordPress, Elementor and WooCommerce for the CIE-4 inter-company course.

Year2025 to 2026
RoleContent and catalogue
ContextCIE-4, group of three
ToolsWordPress, WooCommerce
AI-generated

ConceptThe idea

Every month, subscribers get a box around a theme: Gaming, World Street Food, Art and Design, Outdoor, Tech Gadgets or Wellness. Each box holds 5 to 7 products and a letter telling the story behind the theme.

The target audience: curious 25 to 45 year olds who like being surprised and are looking for an original gift for themselves or someone else.

BuildWhat we set up

  • WordPress with a static homepage and Elementor set to our colours and fonts
  • WooCommerce: at least 5 products with variations, a featured product, promotions and customer reviews
  • Custom shop pages: gallery, product page, cart and checkout
  • Users: an admin, a stock manager with no access to pages, a customer account
  • Content: site copy, company presentation, contact page with form

VisualsLogo and products

The box-shaped MB logo, then realistic AI-generated visuals so the site looks like a real brand: the Outdoor and Adventure box, the Tech Gadgets box and the headquarters in Grandson.
